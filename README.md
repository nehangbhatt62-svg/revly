# Revly

**Real reviews from real customers, via one QR code.**

Revly helps independent food businesses get honest Google reviews from the customers they already have — without dashboards, subscriptions or begging customers at the till.

## How it works

1. The business places a **QR code wherever it suits them** — on the bill, the physical menu, the counter, the order slip, wherever they want.
2. A customer scans it and types what they thought of their visit.
3. Revly's AI turns their raw thoughts into a **well-written review draft** — in whatever language the customer is comfortable in (English, Hindi, Gujarati, and more).
4. The customer **edits it and posts it themselves** — they choose the rating, they choose the words, they decide whether to post. No app, no login, no blank-form drop-off.

Independent venues lose customers every day to competitors with more (and better-written) reviews. Revly is a one-time-setup fix that works on any bill, menu or counter.

## What Revly builds

- **QR Review System** — the QR review flow described above, customisable per business (design, colours, language, layout).
- **Digital Menus** — every dish shows exactly what the business wants it to: veg/non-veg marks, ingredients, spice levels, nutrition, allergen info only if they want it. See a live demo at [menu.reviewwork.uk](https://menu.reviewwork.uk).
- **Websites** — simple, clean sites for food businesses, built in the same style as their menu and QR.

Everything can be customised to exactly how the business wants it — design, colours, language, layout, whatever the business needs.

## Who it's for

Built for independent food businesses: restaurants, cafes, coffee shops, fast food outlets, dessert shops, sweet shops (mithai), ice cream parlours, juice bars, bakeries, food trucks, takeaways and pubs.
